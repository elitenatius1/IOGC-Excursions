Venues & Locations
Two venues will be used this year:

Friday only — Taste of St. Louis:
St. Ann Community Center, #1 Community Center Drive, St. Ann, MO 63074 — approximately 4 miles (7 minutes) from the hotel.

Saturday & Sunday — All remaining events:
Renaissance Hotel St. Louis Airport, 9801 Natural Bridge Rd., St. Louis, MO 63134. The Seminar opens in the Grand Concourse Ballroom on the main floor.
We will occupy most of the hotel, but we are not the only guests. Please be mindful of other guests and ensure that minors are always accompanied by an adult for their safety. Please also treat both venues as your own — use trash receptacles properly and alert Seminar staff to any issues.

Hotel & Accommodations
The nightly rate is $180.00 per room. Check-in is available starting at 3:00 PM at the front desk of the Renaissance Hotel.
All available handicap accessible rooms have been assigned, however not everyone who requested one will be accommodated due to limited availability. Please note that all handicap accessible rooms are doubles — there are no king handicap accessible rooms available.
Your location's housing list has been provided via a link. Please confirm your accommodations and total cost with your local housing representative, who will direct any questions to Deacon Tim Jackson.

Registration & Fees
Seminar check-in takes place on Saturday, May 23 from 7:30 AM – 9:00 AM at the Renaissance Hotel. For more details, visit the National Seminar Website.
All churches are asked to submit their fees and a requested donation of $1,500 per church to either Sister Nina Sims or Deacon Tim Jackson during the event.

If your location has not yet sent its donation, please bring it with you in a separate check.
